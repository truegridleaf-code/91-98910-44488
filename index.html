Our 6-Stage Mechanized Water Tank Cleaning Process

We employ a standardized 6-step mechanized cleaning protocol engineered to eradicate 99.9% of waterborne bacteria, biofilm, and toxic sludge.

01

Mechanized Dewatering

Submersible automatic dewatering pumps quickly drain out stagnant water without flooding your premises.

02

Sludge & Mud Extraction

Heavy slurry vacuum extractors pump out thick mud, sedimentation, and decomposed organic matter from the floor.

03

High-Pressure Rotary Jetting

German high-PSI rotary water jet sprays scour inner concrete and plastic surfaces, eliminating stubborn algae and fungus.

04

Industrial Vacuum Cleaning

Industrial vacuum cleaners suction away all remaining loosened dirt, suspended particulates, and residual water droplets.

05

Food-Grade Anti-Bacterial Spray

Food-grade, certified antibacterial and anti-fungal mist is sprayed to neutralize microscopic pathogens, typhoid, and E. coli.

06

UV Radiation Sterilization

Ultraviolet (UV) radiator lamps are lowered into the empty tank to destroy airborne bacteria and viral spores completely.
